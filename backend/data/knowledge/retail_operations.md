# FreshMart retail operations knowledge

This file is an example company knowledge source for the Retail Edge OS retrieval pipeline. Replace or extend it with approved FreshMart policies, product guides, supplier notes, planograms, and operating procedures.

## Shelf replenishment

Prioritize a shelf when it is marked CRITICAL, when visible units are very low, or when customer demand is likely to exhaust the shelf before the next scheduled check. Confirm backroom stock before assigning a replenishment task. For chilled products, preserve the cold chain and complete the shelf transfer promptly.

## Queue response

When checkout congestion is HIGH or a lane wait materially exceeds nearby open lanes, the shift supervisor should consider opening a standby counter and assigning an available trained associate. The supervisor must confirm staff reassignment before dispatch.

## Incident handling

Critical safety incidents take precedence over inventory and queue optimization. Operational recommendations are proposals until a staff member or supervisor confirms them. The copilot must not claim that a task was completed based only on a recommendation.

## Customer-facing answers

Use current catalog and inventory records for product location, price, and availability. If a requested item is unavailable, suggest alternatives only when the catalog data supplies them. Do not invent promotions, return rules, or availability.
